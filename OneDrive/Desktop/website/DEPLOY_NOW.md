## 🎯 METHOD 1: Netlify Drop (EASIEST - 2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Open Netlify Drop
Go to: **https://app.netlify.com/drop**

### Step 2: Drag Your Folder
- Find your website folder on your computer
- Drag the ENTIRE folder
- Drop it on the Netlify page

### Step 3: Get Your URL
- Netlify will upload and deploy
- You'll get a URL like: `random-name-123.netlify.app`
- **DONE!** Share this URL with everyone!

### Step 4: (Optional) Change URL
- Click "Site settings"
- Click "Change site name"
- Enter: `agmr-notes`
- Your URL becomes: `agmr-notes.netlify.app`

---

## 🎯 METHOD 2: Vercel (Recommended - 5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create Vercel Account
1. Go to: **https://vercel.com**
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Choose "Continue with Email" or "Continue with GitHub"
4. Verify your email

### Step 2: Deploy
1. Click "Add New Project"
2. Click "Browse" or drag your folder
3. Click "Deploy"
4. Wait 1 minute
5. **DONE!** You'll get: `agmr-notes.vercel.app`

## 🆘 If You Face Any Issues

### "Upload Failed"
- Make sure you're dragging the entire folder
- Check internet connection
- Try again

### "Site Not Loading"
- Wait 1-2 minutes after deployment
- Clear browser cache (Ctrl+F5)
- Try different browser

### "Want Custom Domain"
- After deployment, you can add custom domain
- Buy domain from GoDaddy/Namecheap (₹99/year)
- Connect it in Netlify/Vercel settings
